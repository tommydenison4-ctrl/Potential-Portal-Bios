Value Board

Every priced player ranked inside his own position pool — one market per position, up and down sides pooled. Steady value is his benchmark grade’s percentile minus his price’s percentile: what he is now against what he costs. Ceiling value swaps the grade for his band ceiling’s percentile; it runs in tie blocks by construction, breaks its ties by the steady score, and should be read beside the steady column, never alone. His actual NIL band prints beside every score — 79 players sit in bands with a $0 floor, where the midpoint price is half the band ceiling and the score runs high on the model’s cheapest cell. The band is the context the score needs. The 83 players with no NIL estimate (pro-only, no destination to price) are not on the board. A player flagged OVERPAY is priced at least 30 points higher in his position’s price pool than his grade ranks in its grade pool; his player page carries the same note beside the band.
